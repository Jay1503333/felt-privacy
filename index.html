Privacy Policy for Felt

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Felt is a card game for Android. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. This policy explains that in more detail, because Google Play requires every app to publish one.

Information the app collects

None. Felt has no sign-in, no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting SDK. It does not ask for your name, your email address, your contacts, your location, or your device identifiers.

Information the app transmits

None. Felt does not request the Android INTERNET permission, so it has no ability to open a network connection. The only permission it holds is VIBRATE, which produces the small haptic tap when a card moves.

Information stored on your device

Felt saves the following locally, in the app's own private storage, so that your game is still there when you come back:

  • Your current solitaire deal and move history
  • Your settings — deck style, draw count, sound, opponent difficulty and so on
  • Your statistics and achievements — games played, wins, streaks, best time, highest chip count

This data never leaves your device. It is not backed up to any server run by the developer. Uninstalling Felt deletes it permanently.

Children

Felt is not directed to children and is rated for adults. It collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Third parties

Felt contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking libraries, and shares nothing with anyone.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Felt ever collects anything, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
